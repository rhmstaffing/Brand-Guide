# Audience messaging guide

RHM account executives use this when writing client collateral (one-pagers, emails, decks, proposals). Pick the persona first, then write only to what they care about. Cut anything in the "don't care" column.

## Taglines by audience
- **Clients (AE collateral):** Partner with us today to build the workforce that powers your tomorrow.
- **Contractors / job seekers:** Connecting passionate people with unlimited opportunities.
- **Internal hiring:** Turning potential into opportunity, one hire at a time. / Build your future with a team that invests in your success.
- **Brand line (any audience):** The best talent won't come to you, we bring them to you.

## Audience groups
| Group | Persona | Lead with |
|---|---|---|
| Job seeker | Active and passive job hunters | Resume help, interview prep, offer negotiation, career growth |
| Internal hiring | Future recruiters and AEs | Earning potential, career path, high energy culture |
| Client | Hiring Manager | Speed to hire, niche technical talent, less burnout |
| Client | Department Director | Scaling for workload, project spikes, contract to hire flexibility |
| Client | HR / TA | Candidate experience, onboarding readiness, retention |
| Client | Procurement | Vendor consolidation, rate benchmarking, clear costs |
| Client | Finance | Overtime control, temp vs. permanent overhead |
| Client | MSP Program Manager | Compliance, SLAs, speed balanced with quality |
| Client | Executive Sponsor | Strategic headcount, quarterly workforce planning, proactive pipelines |

## Client personas: care / don't care

**Hiring Manager**
- Cares: how fast they get productive people; candidates who show up and perform; less time interviewing unqualified people; solving productivity problems; less overtime and burnout; a partner who makes life easier.
- Doesn't care: your recruiting process (unless it affects speed or quality); recruiter headcount; company history, awards, office locations.

**Department Director**
- Cares: production goals; department performance; labor availability; workforce planning; fewer operational disruptions; whether staffing helps or hurts results.
- Doesn't care: recruiter fill stats; sourcing channels; markup (unless it causes budget problems).

**HR / TA**
- Cares: candidate experience; employer brand; compliance; candidate quality; diversity initiatives; hiring manager satisfaction; time to fill; less workload for their team.
- Doesn't care: sales call volume; sales awards; generic "better than the competition" claims.

**Procurement**
- Cares: contract compliance; supplier performance; risk management; competitive pricing; SLAs; insurance; legal protection; supplier diversity; standardization.
- Doesn't care: your relationship with the hiring manager; emotional candidate stories; how hard recruiting has become.

**Finance**
- Cares: total labor cost; budget predictability; ROI; cost of vacancies; overtime; invoice accuracy; financial risk.
- Doesn't care: recruiting techniques or technology; how hard recruiting is.

**MSP Program Manager**
- Cares: cost control; fill-rate requirements; response times; VMS compliance; candidate quality; SLA performance; supplier scorecards; accurate reporting; low issue rates; consistency.
- Doesn't care: being a "favorite vendor"; your culture; recruiter headcount.

**Executive Sponsor**
- Cares: business growth; workforce strategy; risk reduction; retention; productivity; strategic partnerships; revenue; customer satisfaction; competitive advantage; long-term workforce planning.
- Doesn't care: individual job orders; recruiter workloads; your software; resume counts; staffing jargon.

## Writing rules for collateral
- One persona per piece. If you have to cover two, lead with the more senior one.
- Headline = their outcome, in their words ("Control your labor costs", not "Our flexible staffing model").
- 3 value points max. Each point: short title + one sentence of proof.
- Proof from RHM facts only: 4-step screening, E-Verify / workers' comp / drug and background checks, salary benchmarks, weekly touchpoints, no large upfront fees, 7 industries, 3 hiring models. Don't invent stats.
- Same voice rules as the rest of the brand: no emoji, no em dashes, short sentences, "we" and "you".
- End with one clear ask and a named contact.
